# Design decisions

Calls that generalise, with their why, so nobody re-litigates them. Newest first. A decision that
becomes a rule also lands in `design-standard.md`. Open items carry ◇.

| 2026-10-27 | A tray's card, content and CTA are one SwiftUI view on one spring (`TrayMotion.resize`), inside an invisible system sheet (`.presentationBackground(.clear)` plus a clear detent `backgroundEffect`, iOS 26.1+).
