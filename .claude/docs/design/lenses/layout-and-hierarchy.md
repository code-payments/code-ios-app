# Layout and hierarchy lens

This lens judges whether a screen has one thing to look at, one thing to do, and a rhythm that makes everything else recede: hierarchy, spacing, alignment, surfaces (cards, sheets, glass, rings, radii), empty states, layout stability, and icons. The reviewer's posture is subtract first and squint last: walk every element and ask what breaks if it is gone, then blur the screen and ask whether it could belong to any of ten thousand other apps; if only the logo ties it to Flipcash, it fails, however clean each piece is. Paths below: `UI/` is `FlipcashUI/Sources/FlipcashUI/`, `Screens/` is `Flipcash/Core/Screens/`.

## Rules

1. **Give a screen one `.buttonStyle(.filled)` per rendered state; every other action takes `.subtle`, `.textLink`, `.filled05`, or `.icon(_:)`.** Two white 54 pt capsules make neither of them the next step. `.subtle` is the one secondary; on a full screen the secondary under a primary can be a `.textLink`. (Revised 2026-11-19: `.filled20` is deleted.) `Screens/Settings/Withdraw/WithdrawIntroScreen.swift:38-39`; the ladder is `UI/Views/ButtonStyles/FilledButtonStyle.swift:82-113`, `SubtleButtonStyle.swift:64-71` and `TextLinkButtonStyle.swift`.
2. **On any screen that shows an amount, set the amount in the largest font on the screen and keep every other text at `.appDisplaySmall` (24) or below.** Money is why the screen exists, and a title at the same size competes with it. `Screens/Main/Home/BalanceHeaderButton.swift:30` (`.appDisplayLarge`, 55).
3. **Build hierarchy by demoting, not by adding: section headings are `.appTextHeading` in `.textSecondary`, supporting lines are `.textSecondary`, and only the hero and the primary action are full white.** Making a second thing loud makes nothing loud. `Screens/Settings/SettingsAdvancedBetaFeaturesScreen.swift:124-134`.
4. **Delete any element that nothing breaks without, then walk the screen again before styling what is left.** A caption that restates its heading, an icon on every label, and a divider that spacing already draws are the additive tells of generated UI, and deletion cannot introduce new ones. `Screens/Settings/SettingsMyAccountScreen.swift:43-71` (icon, title, separator; nothing else).
5. **Lay out on the rhythm: 20 for screen gutters and the primary button's inset, 12 between siblings, 8 inside a component, 4 between a label and its caption, with 16, 24, and 40 as the section steps.** The rhythm is what makes unrelated screens read as one app; 14, 28, and 30 are drift everywhere, 10 and 15 are drift outside the chat transcript (which keeps its own 5/10/15 row rhythm), and a new scale is a system decision, not a per-screen one. A FlipcashUI component that mirrors an Android spec value states that in its `///` doc; otherwise the rhythm binds components too. `Screens/Main/Home/WalletScreen.swift:549,557,582,590`.
6. **Space siblings with the stack's `spacing:`, never with `.padding(.bottom, n)` on each child.** Per-child padding leaves a trailing gap after the last item and hides the rhythm from the next reader. `Screens/Main/Home/WalletScreen.swift:589-611`.
7. **Pin a full screen's bottom actions with a `Spacer()` above them, 20 pt at the sides and top, and `.bottomEdgePadding()` for the bottom: 20 pt from the device's bottom edge, not from the home-indicator safe area. A screen that ends in a `.textLink` takes `.bottomEdgePaddingUnderTextLink()` (32 pt under the link's 44 pt box).** The last button then sits as far from the bottom as from the sides, and the keyboard still lifts it. Two buttons together sit `Metrics.buttonGap` (12) apart. (Revised 2026-11-19: replaces `.safeAreaInset` plus 20 pt bottom padding.) `Screens/Main/DownloadAppScreen.swift:46-53`; `Screens/Settings/Withdraw/WithdrawIntroScreen.swift:36-42`; `Screens/Onboarding/NotificationPermissionScreen.swift:52-57`.
8. **Clear the tab bar with a measured inset, branched on OS: 40 on iOS 26, 40 plus the legacy pill clearance on iOS 18.** The iOS 26 bar sits in the safe area and the iOS 18 pill floats over content, so one constant is wrong on one of them. `Screens/Main/Home/WalletScreen.swift:129-140`.
9. **Align every label and icon to one leading edge per screen; center only a hero (a balance, an illustration) or a confirmation.** Two alignments on one screen read as two layouts pasted together. `Screens/Settings/SettingsMyAccountScreen.swift:43`; `Screens/Settings/Withdraw/WithdrawIntroScreen.swift:31-34`.
10. **Reserve a fixed leading slot for row icons, sized to the neighbouring rows' leading element (45 pt in Settings, 44 pt beside avatars), so titles share one left edge whatever the glyph's width.** Glyphs vary by up to 8 pt in width, and titles that drift with them look ragged. `Screens/Settings/SettingsRow.swift:38`.
11. **Let wrapping labels grow with `.fixedSize(horizontal: false, vertical: true)` and give a pushing `Spacer` a `minLength`.** Without them a long title truncates to one line or crushes its neighbor to zero width. `Screens/Main/Home/WalletScreen.swift:632-637`.
12. **Make depth with fills and hairline rings, not shadows: a card is `.backgroundRow` (white 5%), a tappable tile is white 10%, and the ring is `.rowSeparator` (white 10%) at 1 pt via `strokeBorder`.** On #19191A a drop shadow is invisible and a bright stroke glows, while a translucent ring composites on any fill. `Screens/Main/Home/TokenCardView.swift:98-101`; `Flipcash/Views/TutorialChecklistCard.swift:59-60`.
13. **Leave `.shadow(` off every card, row, tile, and sheet; the one lifted surface is the chat bubble under its context menu, black at 0.135, radius 20, offset y 10.** One lift in the whole app means lift reads as a state, not a style. `UI/Chat/BubbleBackgroundView.swift:222-240`.
14. **Separate rows with `vSeparator` in `.rowSeparator` (white 10%, 1 px), never with a 1 pt line or a second fill.** A 1 px rule recedes on a 3x screen; a 1 pt line reads as a border and a second fill as a card. `UI/Modifiers/Separator.swift:68-73`; `UI/Views/Containers/Row.swift:52`.
15. **Use three radii only, plus `Capsule` for buttons, pills, chips and toasts (a circle for an icon-only button): `Metrics.buttonRadius` (6) for fields, tiles, and small cards, never a button; `Metrics.boxRadius` (12) for boxes, sheets, and the bill card; 12 with 4 on grouped inner corners for chat bubbles.** Each extra radius is one more thing that looks undecided. (Revised 2026-11-19: buttons are capsules.) `UI/Metrics.swift:23-24`; `UI/Chat/BubbleBackgroundView.swift:31-32,279-287`.
16. **Corners are continuous.** SwiftUI's `RoundedRectangle(cornerRadius:)`, `UnevenRoundedRectangle` and `.rect(cornerRadius:)` default to `.continuous` (iOS SDK interface), so `style: .continuous` is optional and `style: .circular` is a finding (none today). In UIKit, `layer.cornerRadius` draws a circular corner unless `layer.cornerCurve = .continuous` sits beside it. `Screens/Main/Home/TokenCardView.swift:90-99`; `FlipcashUI/Chat/BubbleBackgroundView.swift:22`.
17. **Derive a nested radius as the outer radius minus the padding between the two surfaces, and make the inner surface square when that reaches 0.** A child that copies its parent's radius pinches at the corners; the tokens allow exactly one derivation, a 6 inside a 12 with 6 pt between. A capsule button inside a card is exempt and needn't be concentric with it. `UI/Metrics.swift:23-24`.
18. **Put glass only over content that moves beneath it (a scrolling list, a photo, the bill), through `glassBackground(cornerRadius:)`, `capsuleGlassBackground()`, or `liquidGlassButtonStyle(shape:)`, grouped in a `GlassContainer` when two pieces sit together.** Glass over a flat `backgroundMain` is blur with nothing to blur and reads as a costume; the container lets neighbors share one sample. `UI/Views/Containers/GlassBackground.swift:12-18`; `Screens/Main/Home/WalletScreen.swift:815-823`.
19. **Ship every glass surface with its iOS 18 fallback: `.ultraThinMaterial` in the same shape, `.thinMaterial` for a clear sheet.** The fallback is the design on every iOS 18 device, not an afterthought. `UI/Views/Containers/GlassBackground.swift:16,44`; `UI/Views/Containers/PartialSheet.swift:55-66`.
20. **Present partial-height sheets with `PartialSheet`, whose fill is `.backgroundSecondary` (#252526), one step lighter than the screen.** The sheet reads as a layer because it is lighter; a sheet painted `backgroundMain` merges into the screen behind it. `UI/Views/Containers/PartialSheet.swift:20`.
21. **Give a pressed button one feedback, the house press: a shrink to 0.95 on `Metrics.buttonPressAnimation`, or a dim to 0.7 under Reduce Motion, through `ButtonPress`.** Two feedbacks on one press (scale plus opacity, or scale plus glass) stack and feel laggy. Tiles, rows and cards keep their own press, icon-only circles keep a dim, and picker segments take none; one dim value for the circles is an open decision in `design-decisions.md`. (Revised 2026-11-19: the shrink replaces the opacity drop.) `UI/Views/ButtonStyles/ButtonPress.swift:24-28`; `UI/Views/ButtonStyles/FilledButtonStyle.swift:68`; `UI/Views/ButtonStyles/SubtleButtonStyle.swift:50`.
22. **Size buttons with `Metrics.buttonHeight` (54) and `Metrics.buttonHeightThin` (44), never with a literal.** A literal cannot follow the token when it changes, and 54 written by hand is indistinguishable from a guess. A title stays on one line at that height: it shrinks to 0.8, then truncates. (Revised 2026-11-19: 54, was 60.) `UI/Views/ButtonStyles/FilledButtonStyle.swift:57`.
23. **Give every tappable glyph and text pill (a chip, a toast action) a 44 pt hit area with `.frame(minHeight: 44)` and a `.contentShape(...)`, and never let two hit areas overlap.** Without `contentShape` only the glyph's pixels take the tap; overlapping areas fire the wrong control. `Screens/Main/Home/WalletScreen.swift:342-345`.
24. **Size an empty state like its filled state: a headline in `.appTextLarge`, one `.textSecondary` line, and the action that fills it, via `ContentUnavailableView` or `SearchResultsUnavailableView`.** A `Spacer()`-only branch is a blank screen that jumps when content arrives. `UI/Views/SearchResultsUnavailableView.swift:22-36`; `Screens/Settings/SettingsAdvancedBetaFeaturesScreen.swift:39-47`.
25. **Hold loading layout with skeleton rows the size of real rows (`.redacted(reason: .placeholder)`) and seed state synchronously so first paint shows real data.** A spinner collapses the page and the arriving rows shove everything down; a default-then-correct render flashes. `Screens/Main/Currency Discovery/CurrencyDiscoverySkeletonRow.swift:47`; `Screens/Main/Home/WalletScreen.swift:146-149`.
26. **Stop numbers jiggling: `.monospacedDigit()` on any figure that updates in place, `.contentTransition(.numericText())` on any figure that animates.** Proportional digits change width per digit and the whole row shifts on every tick. `Screens/Main/Currency Discovery/CurrencyDiscoveryRow.swift:22`; `Screens/Main/Home/BalanceHeaderButton.swift:32`.
27. **Reference icons through `Image.system(_:)` with a `SystemSymbol` case or `Image.asset(_:)` with an `Asset` case, never `Image(systemName: "...")` with a string and never an emoji in a `Text`.** The enums are the house's icon inventory, a string bypasses it, and emoji take no tint and render differently per OS. `UI/Theme/Image+Symbols.swift:13-19`.
28. **Use one icon style per list, SF Symbols or the asset set, and reach for the asset set only where SF Symbols has no glyph.** Mixed stroke weights down one list read as two products. `Screens/Settings/SettingsMyAccountScreen.swift:18-22`; `Screens/Main/Home/WalletScreen.swift:592-594`.
29. **Size a symbol by its font and an asset by a 24 pt frame, and match the glyph's weight to the adjacent text's weight.** A thin glyph beside demi text looks like a mistake. `Screens/Main/Home/WalletScreen.swift:649-662`.
30. **Set type only with `Font.app*` tokens; `.font(.system(` on a `Text` skips the house weight mapping and Dynamic Type scaling.** The tokens map bold to Semibold and scale with the body style; a raw system font does neither, and `.default(size:)` with a literal is a token nobody else can find. `UI/Theme/AppFonts.swift:12-37`; `UI/Theme/FontBook.swift:108-110`.
31. **Finish with the squint test: blur the screen and name what ties it to Flipcash; if the answer is only the logo, it fails.** The bill-colored card, the 55 pt balance, and the 6 pt continuous tile are the house's signature; a row of three icon-title-caption cards or a centered pill badge over a headline belongs to ten thousand other apps. `Screens/Main/Home/WalletScreen.swift:496-558`.

## Flipcash specifics

- Theme: one dark mode. `backgroundMain` #19191A, `backgroundSecondary` #252526, `backgroundRow` white 5%, `rowSeparator` white 10%, `textMain` white, `textSecondary` (123, 123, 123), `textTertiary` for placeholder amounts. There is no light theme to design for. `UI/Theme/Colors.swift:14-41`.
- Rhythm as practiced in `Screens/`: `.padding(20)` x48, 12 x7, 16 x6, 24 x5; edge padding 20 x116, 12 x34, 8 x26, 40 x21, 16 x19; `spacing:` 12 x38, 8 x31, 4 x21, 20 x15, 16 x12. The stragglers (10 x22, 15 x13, 14, 28, 30) are drift, not steps. No spacing scale exists yet; adding one is a system decision.
- Chat transcript rhythm is its own: row gaps 5 inside a run, 10 between runs, 15 on a speaker change; bubble body padding 9/12; bubble radius 12 with 4 on grouped inner corners. Judge the transcript against the chat spec (`.claude/plans/2026-09-29-figma-component-library.md`, chat pass), not the screen rhythm.
- Radii: buttons are capsules (icon-only buttons circles) and never read `Metrics.buttonRadius`. `Metrics.buttonRadius` 6 (40 uses, counted before the 2026-11-19 capsule change) and `Metrics.boxRadius` 12 (5 uses: `TokenCardView`, `ConversationGatePanel`, `CardButtonStyle`); literal 6 ×14 and 12 ×5 across screens and `UI/`. Chat bubbles are 12 with 4 on grouped corners. 42 call sites pass `style: .continuous`; that is the house's corner, even where older styles still draw circular ones. `UI/Metrics.swift:23-24`.
- Heights: `Metrics.buttonHeight` 54, `buttonHeightThin` 44, `buttonGap` 12, `buttonPadding` 20, `buttonLineWidth` 1; `buttonPaddingUnderTextLink` 32 under a screen-ending text link. `UI/Metrics.swift:18-26`; `UI/Views/ButtonStyles/BottomEdgePadding.swift`.
- Button ladder, all capsules: `.filled` (white fill, black text) is the one primary; `.subtle` (white 10%, `Color.actionSecondary`) is the one secondary; `.textLink` (no fill, text at 60%, 44 pt) for a help or escape link, or the secondary under a primary on a full screen; `.filled05` (the row tint, for an action that reads as a row); `.icon(_:)` (white 10% with an 18 pt asset); `.card(icon:)` for tiles; `.filledCompact` and `.subtleCompact` at 44. Pressed is the house press, 0.95 over a 0.2 s spring. `UI/Views/ButtonStyles/FilledButtonStyle.swift:82-113`; `UI/Views/ButtonStyles/ButtonPress.swift`.
- Screen skeleton: `Background(color: .backgroundMain)` > `ScrollView(showsIndicators: false)` > `VStack(spacing: 0)` > `.padding(.horizontal, 20)`. `Screens/Settings/SettingsMyAccountScreen.swift:31-36`; `Screens/Main/Home/WalletScreen.swift:497-557`.
- Rows: `Row(insets:)` lays an `HStack(spacing: 12)` over a bottom `rowSeparator`; settings rows use `EdgeInsets(top: 25, bottom: 25)`, a 45 pt icon slot, and `.appDisplayXS` titles. `UI/Views/Containers/Row.swift:30-53`; `Screens/Settings/SettingsMyAccountScreen.swift:28,72`.
- Cards: `.backgroundRow` fill, `Metrics.buttonRadius` continuous, `.padding(16)` inside; the bill card is `boxRadius` with a white 10% ring; `BorderedContainer` (radius 12, `textSecondary` 30% stroke over `backgroundRow`) groups inputs. `Flipcash/Views/TutorialChecklistCard.swift:50-60`; `UI/Views/Containers/BorderedContainer.swift:28-34`.
- Glass: `glassBackground(cornerRadius:)`, `glassBackground(cornerRadius:tint:)`, `capsuleGlassBackground()`, `glassFieldBackground(cornerRadius:)` (text fields; keep it out of a `GlassContainer`), `GlassContainer(spacing:)`, `liquidGlassButtonStyle(shape:)`, `prominentButtonStyle()`. iOS 18 falls back to `.ultraThinMaterial`; the circle button falls back to a white 7% fill with a 10% ring. `.glassEffect(.interactive())` goes inside a `ButtonStyle`, never as a modifier on a `Button` (reflection 2026-02-27). `UI/Views/Containers/GlassBackground.swift:8-63`; `UI/Views/ButtonStyles/LiquidGlassCompatibleButtonStyle.swift:15-89`.
- Sheets: `PartialSheet` sizes itself to content on `.backgroundSecondary`; `.clear` falls back to `.thinMaterial` on iOS 18. `UI/Views/Containers/PartialSheet.swift:11-47`.
- Hairlines: `vSeparator(color:position:weight:)`; `.regular` is 1 px (`1 / displayScale`), `.medium` is 1 pt. `UI/Modifiers/Separator.swift:64-74`.
- Empty and loading: `SearchResultsUnavailableView`, a themed `ContentUnavailableView`, `CurrencyDiscoverySkeletonRow`, `Loadable`; toasts go through `FloatingToast` and `ToastController`, never a hand-built overlay.
- Icons: the `SystemSymbol` and `Asset` enums are the inventory; Android-matched assets (`profilePicture`, `myAccount`, `advanced`, `deposit`, `withdraw`) exist where SF Symbols has no glyph. `UI/Theme/Image+Symbols.swift:46,104`.
- Money: `BalanceHeaderButton` sets the total at `.appDisplayLarge` (55) with `.numericText()`; `AmountText` pairs a flag with an amount. `Screens/Main/Home/BalanceHeaderButton.swift:30-32`; `UI/Views/AmountText.swift:12`.
- Reuse before build: a component in `.claude/docs/design/components.md` (`Row`, `SettingsRow`, `PartialSheet`, `BorderedContainer`, `InputContainer`, `LazyTable`, `Badge`, `BadgedIcon`, `Chip`, `.pill()`, `ImmutableField`, `CapsuleButton`, `ButtonStateLabel`, `FloatingToast`) before a hand-rolled stack.

## Flag on sight

- Two `.buttonStyle(.filled)` reachable in one rendered state of a body (separate `switch` arms or `if/else` branches are fine): rule 1.
- `.shadow(` on a card, row, tile, sheet, or any view with a `backgroundRow` fill: rule 13.
- `RoundedRectangle(cornerRadius: 12)` or `RoundedRectangle(cornerRadius: Metrics.boxRadius)` without `style: .continuous`: rule 16.
- `cornerRadius: 8`, `10`, `16`, `20`, or any literal other than 6, 12, and 4: rule 15.
- `.padding(13)`, `.padding(.top, 15)`, `spacing: 10`, `spacing: 14`, or any value outside 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 40 in a screen file or in a component with no documented spec value (the chat transcript's 5/10/15 row gaps are its own rhythm): rule 5.
- `.padding(.bottom, n)` repeated on each child of a `VStack(spacing: 0)`: rule 6.
- `Color(red:`, `Color(hex:`, `Color(r:`, or `Color.white.opacity(0.05)` in a screen file where `.backgroundRow` or another token fits: rule 12.
- `Color.backgroundMain` as the fill of a sheet, card, or tile: rule 20.
- `.glassEffect(` or `glassBackground(` on a view whose only backdrop is `backgroundMain`: rule 18.
- `if #available(iOS 26` around a glass call with no `else` branch: rule 19.
- `.frame(height: 54)`, `.frame(height: 60)` or `.frame(height: 44)` on a button instead of `Metrics.buttonHeight` or `Metrics.buttonHeightThin`: rule 22.
- An icon-only `Button` with no `.frame(width: 44, height: 44)` and no `.contentShape(`: rule 23.
- `if items.isEmpty { Spacer() }`, `EmptyView()`, or a bare `Text("No items")` as the empty branch: rule 24.
- `ProgressView()` as the whole loading state of a list: rule 25.
- `Image(systemName: "...")` with a string literal in a screen file; `Text("🔥")` or any emoji literal standing in for an icon: rule 27.
- `.font(.system(` on a `Text`; `.font(.default(size:` with a literal size in a screen file: rule 30.
- `.scaleEffect(configuration.isPressed` or `.opacity(configuration.isPressed` in a titled button's style instead of `.buttonPress(isPressed:)`: rule 21.
- `RoundedRectangle(cornerRadius: Metrics.buttonRadius)` or `.rect(cornerRadius: 6)` as a button's shape: rule 15.
- `.padding(.bottom, 20)` or `.safeAreaInset(edge: .bottom)` holding a full screen's bottom actions instead of `.bottomEdgePadding()`: rule 7.
- Three equal tiles each with icon, title, and caption; a centered pill badge above a headline; a gradient with no job: rule 31.

## Needs a device

- Dynamic Type at AX3 through AX5 (`Font.default` is `Font.custom(_:size:)`, so every token scales): open Wallet, My Account, and a `PartialSheet`; the balance must not clip, a 54 pt button's label must stay on one line (it shrinks to 0.8, then truncates), and no two 44 pt hit areas may overlap. `UI/Theme/FontBook.swift:108-110`.
- The longest real username and display name, and a long token name, on a `SettingsRow`, the `TokenCardView` header, and a chat bubble: the balance stays visible and the `Spacer(minLength:)` holds.
- iOS 18 on a device without glass: the `.ultraThinMaterial` open-card chrome, the floating tab bar, a `.clear` `PartialSheet`, and the wallet's legacy pill clearance at the bottom of the scroll. `Screens/Main/Home/HomeTabView.swift:261`.
- iOS 26: every glass surface has moving content beneath it, and grouped glass sits in one `GlassContainer` so neighbors share a sample.
- Press feedback: tap each button style and confirm one feedback (the 0.95 shrink, or the 0.7 dim with Reduce Motion on) with no glass animation stacked on top; a screenshot cannot show this.
- Scroll edges: content scrolling into the status bar fades (the wallet draws its own fade because it hides its nav bar), and the last row clears the tab bar on both OS versions. `Screens/Main/Home/WalletScreen.swift:565-578`.
- Hairlines: a `.regular` separator renders as one device pixel on a 3x screen, not a blurred 2 px line.
- Landscape and iPad are out of scope: the target is iPhone only, portrait only (`TARGETED_DEVICE_FAMILY = 1`, `UISupportedInterfaceOrientations = Portrait` in `Code.xcodeproj/project.pbxproj`).
- The squint test itself: hold the device at arm's length, or blur a screenshot, and name the one thing a competitor could not paste.
