## Root cause

Leading hypothesis. The group's "notFound" title comes from its latest event. The group itself holds every capture from the catch-all in `receiveCashLink`: `captureError` without an `id:` (`Session.swift:1914`) hashes as `Session.swift:receiveCashLink(...)` only (`ErrorReporting.swift:207-211`). Of the 411 events, 103 (17 users) are `ErrorFetchBalance.notFound`, and they already report at `.info` (`AccountInfoService.swift:197`), so they need no reclassification. All 223 error-severity events (37 users) are `ErrorSubmitIntent.grpcStatus(13)`, gRPC INTERNAL, returned to the client in the server's "rpc server failure" response. 211 of them have a group-chat cash link in the user's recent logs, so the leading hypothesis is that the server answers some losing claims with INTERNAL instead of `staleState(alreadyClaimed)`. The server logs needed to confirm it are missing.

## Evidence

- nserror split across all 411 events: grpcStatus(13) 223 error · notFound 103 info · transportFailure 66 info · staleState 14 info · invalidIntent 3 info · grpcStatus(14) 2 info
- log `[2026-10-26 23:48:33] WARNING transaction-service`: "Intent stream closed with non-OK status code=internalError message=rpc server failure". All 223 have this line, on `IntentReceiveCashLink` (216) or `IntentCreateAccount` (7).
- 211/223 logs carry a `CHAT_MESSAGE_GROUP` push or a `send.flipcash.com/c/#/e=` link. 152 also logged "Cash Already Collected" or "not available" within the same 100 lines.
- The INTERNAL events land in bursts: 17 on 09-30, 21 on 10-03, 22 on 10-06, 29 on 10-09, 30 on 10-14, 31 on 10-15, 19 on 10-19, against 1–4 on most other days. 207 distinct gift-card vaults. Top users: 29, 27, 21.
- Intent ids for the backend: `Jzn9…hP6D` 2026-10-26 23:48:32Z, `5t48…BU9m` 2026-10-14 23:40:38Z, `ELp7…fRFc` 2026-10-04 05:15:50Z, `Yfgs…yPFF` 2026-09-03 15:25:14Z
- notFound: of 25 missing gift-card keys, 1 matched the 355 links I could derive from logs. It was a $0.25 link posted at 18:39:33 on 2026-09-28 in the group "Moony". Two users fetched it 16 s and 48–65 s later and got notFound after the 3-attempt, 500 ms retry (`Session.swift:1751-1765`). This sample can't say whether the link was ever funded. `ocp_account_service.proto:84` defines `NOT_FOUND = 1` with no comment on when the server returns it.

## Proposed direction

Leave notFound at `.info`. It is an expected outcome, whether the link was never funded or the server has not caught up yet. Split the bucket by passing `id: "\(type(of: error))"` (or the error case) to the catch-all `captureError`. The INTERNAL events then form their own group and stop wearing the notFound title. Take the INTERNAL question to the backend with the intent ids above: does a losing concurrent claim return INTERNAL?

## Verification

After the split, a notFound capture and a grpcStatus(13) capture from `receiveCashLink` produce different `groupingHash` values (unit test on the hash builder). Backend answers the INTERNAL question.

## Risk

The id change re-buckets every future event from this function, so the existing group stops receiving events and should be closed when the build ships.
