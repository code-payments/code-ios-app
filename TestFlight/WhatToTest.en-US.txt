What to test in this build

New in this release:
• Request money from a group with a different amount for each person
• Create a private group and share its invite link right away
• As a host, restrict or remove a member from the Members list
• Send money to a group: every member receives the amount you entered
• Cash messages in chat all show the same details
• Payment requests sit under the top bar, with pinned messages below them
• Request Money is listed above Send Money in the group money sheet
• System messages in chat show no info icon; the pinned message bar has a lighter tint
• Invite People opens with your invite link, not a blank sheet
• A pinned reply shows the name of the person it quotes
• Send a photo with transparency: the transparent parts show white, matching the upload

Baseline — always verify these core money flows:
• Claim a Cash Link on an empty account, then buy a currency with the received balance
• Add money to your balance (Phantom or Coinbase)
• Scan & Send between two devices
• Expand a chat notification — the rich transcript renders and Reply works

Report anything off — wrong amounts, stuck screens, or crashes.
