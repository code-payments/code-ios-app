What to test in this build

New in this release:
• Send paid reactions in direct messages and group chats.
• Chats Search lets you find conversations quickly.
• Scanning a group invite link shows a card to join that group.
• Converting from Dollars no longer charges a fee.
• Swaps and Phantom deposits now finish in place before processing.
• Creating a community chat now requires holding a community currency.
• The Wallet tab is now called Money, and its tiles and screens have clearer names.
• Tapping a currency in search opens it on the Money tab.
• The paid reaction sheet is now titled "Boost Reaction."
• Tapping a reply quote now takes you back to where you were before jumping to the bottom of the chat.
• Pick a new group's currency from a full-width row.
• The app reconnects to the server when your network changes.
• The send-cash button stays hidden while photos are staged in a chat.
• Mentions show as highlighted pills in photo and media captions.
• Chats update promptly when a newer message arrives.
• Private group and encrypted chat notifications name the sender and preview photos.
• A payment that opens a direct message is now recorded as a send, unless it came from a Flipcard scan.
• The start-chatting fee stays on the sheet while the send completes.
• The keyboard closes before a currency opens from search.
• The scan screen's gallery button now sits in the toolbar.
• System notices now update your read position.
• Join notices name the people they're about.
• Removed a duplicate Leaderboard heading in Discovery.
• Fixed a rounding error in some amounts.

Baseline — always verify these core money flows:
• Claim a Cash Link on an empty account, then buy a currency with the received balance
• Add money to your balance (Phantom or Coinbase)
• Scan & Send between two devices
• Expand a chat notification — the rich transcript renders and Reply works

Report anything off — wrong amounts, stuck screens, or crashes.
