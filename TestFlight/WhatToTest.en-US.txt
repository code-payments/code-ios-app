What to test in this build

New in this release — please exercise these end to end:
• Launch the app — it should open on the Chat tab
• React to a message from the reaction strip and by double-tapping it; open the emoji sheet and long-press for skin tones
• Mention someone with an @handle and tap it — it should open their profile
• Send cash into a group chat — it should arrive as a cash link card
• Create a group through the new invite flow; every group should start with its invite card
• Create a group that requires a balance across all currencies, then join one by buying exactly the required amount
• Open a group you qualify for but haven't joined — you should be able to read the conversation
• From a gated group chat, buy, deposit, or convert — you should land back in that chat
• Open a group member's profile and tap Message
• Open a person link and a tip card link — each should show a person card and open their profile
• Tap a link in a message that leaves Flipcash — you should be warned first
• Swipe a chat in the list to mute it
• Check unread counts: each chat row, the Chats tab badge (groups included, capped at 99+), and the divider where unread messages start
• Scan a group invite QR and open a Flipcash universal link from outside the app — each should land in the right place
• Send a DM with a fee in a non-USD region — the minimum should respect the regional tip minimum

Baseline — always verify these core money flows:
• Claim a Cash Link on an empty account, then buy a currency with the received balance
• Add money to your balance (Phantom or Coinbase)
• Scan & Send between two devices
• Expand a chat notification — the rich transcript renders and Reply works

Report anything off — wrong amounts, stuck screens, or crashes.
