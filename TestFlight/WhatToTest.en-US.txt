What to test in this build

New in this release:
• Reply to a message in chat, then tap the quote on the reply to jump back to the original
• Edit or delete one of your chat messages and check the transcript updates for the other person
• Tap an activity row to open its details; cancel a pending cash link from the Details screen
• Set a profile picture and check it shows as the You tab icon and in My Account
• Work through the Finish Your Profile checklist on the You tab
• Set a minimum tip in My Account, then have someone open a chat with you by tipping
• Buy a currency from its own info screen
• Receive a grabbed deposit and watch it land in the wallet
• Send, give, buy and convert amount screens share one left-aligned layout
• Change a profile field and confirm the confirmation dialog before it saves
• Enter an amount equal to the displayed maximum and check it is accepted
• Enter a username with a leading @ and check it is accepted
• Tap anywhere on the Send Cash pill in a chat

Baseline — always verify these core money flows:
• Claim a Cash Link on an empty account, then buy a currency with the received balance
• Add money to your balance (Phantom or Coinbase)
• Scan & Send between two devices
• Expand a chat notification — the rich transcript renders and Reply works

Report anything off — wrong amounts, stuck screens, or crashes.
