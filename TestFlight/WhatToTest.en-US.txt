What to test in this build

New in this release:
• Create invite links for your group, with usage limits from 1 to No limit, and open group invite links in the app.
• Pin and unpin messages, and see pinned messages at the top of the chat.
• Group hosts can restrict and remove members.
• Edit a group's join and chat balance requirements from Edit Group.
• Get an in-app alert when someone reacts to or boosts your message.
• Ask a private group for money and pay your share, or send cash to picked members.
• Enter a total on a payment request and split it between people.
• Payment requests stay available offline.
• Check your notification status from a new page in Settings.
• Resume onboarding if the app closes partway through, or when you log back in.
• Currency pages show the community leader and let you send, open community chats, and share to chats.
• Tap a photo to show controls for reacting, replying, saving, and forwarding.
• See when a message was sent by dragging it or from the message menu.
• Every amount appears in your display currency.
• Boosted payments are titled in your history and link to the boosted message.
• Chat date separators appear after a one-hour pause.
• The chat list loads and resumes more smoothly.
• The home tab is named Wallet again.
• Usernames can be up to 30 characters.
• Fixed a crash on iOS 18 when editing an older draft.
• A Cash Link claim that loses the race now shows as already collected.
• Photo captions render like text messages.
• The paid-reaction pill shows gold only when you reacted.
• Jumping to a message now centers it on screen.
• The keyboard closes before opening join requests.
• Chat search starts fresh each time you open it.
• The share extension can open private group keys.
• The app recovers when a server connection reset leaves requests stuck.

Baseline — always verify these core money flows:
• Claim a Cash Link on an empty account, then buy a currency with the received balance
• Add money to your balance (Phantom or Coinbase)
• Scan & Send between two devices
• Expand a chat notification — the rich transcript renders and Reply works

Report anything off — wrong amounts, stuck screens, or crashes.
