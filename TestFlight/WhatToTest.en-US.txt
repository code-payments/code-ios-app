What to test in this build

New in this release:
• Share a payment request, and see marked-paid and closed requests in the chat.
• When cash arrives in an open chat, the bill it came with shows right there.
• Payment requests have a Send Request button, and the Name field starts empty.
• New Chat lists groups first and opens the new chat as soon as it is created.
• Mark chats as unread or read from the chat list.
• Send back the opening payment in a direct message.
• Save chat photos, send several as a stack, and edit their captions.
• Tap an @mention in a group to open that member's details.
• Ended payment requests leave the list.
• If you have no balance, paying a request asks you to add money first.
• The Invite People sheet is slimmer.
• Cash bubbles have tighter spacing, and cash cards fade in details that arrive late.
• Scrolling is smoother while you read unread messages.
• Launch, group sends, and opening screens are faster.
• The app download is smaller.
• Chats move to their new place in the list before the list appears on resume.
• Groups you just joined or left stay that way when an older update arrives.
• Older chat history no longer disappears when the newest messages load.
• No stray placeholder shapes appear behind the group-ready card.
• The Archived row counts muted chats.
• The @flipcash chat no longer appears in send recipients.
• Group send shows "each" correctly, and the pay sheet keeps its amount until it closes.
• The group request people picker is titled "Request From."
• A new 7-day request no longer shows as 8 days.

Baseline — always verify these core money flows:
• Claim a Cash Link on an empty account, then buy a currency with the received balance
• Add money to your balance (Phantom or Coinbase)
• Scan & Send between two devices
• Expand a chat notification — the rich transcript renders and Reply works

Report anything off — wrong amounts, stuck screens, or crashes.
