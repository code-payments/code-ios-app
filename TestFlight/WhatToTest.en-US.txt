What to test in this build

New in this release — verify each end to end:
• Create private groups.
• Share photos and text into chats from other apps.
• Paste or drop images into the message composer.
• Forward a message through the share sheet.
• Links in messages show a preview card with the page's image and title.
• Links to messages open the linked message and appear as a message card.
• Jump to the newest message with a button that shows how many you missed.
• Skip the leaving-Flipcash warning for sites you trust.
• Flipcash tells you when a newer version is on the App Store, checking each time you return to the app.
• Settings shows your app version and flags when an update is available.
• Trusted Websites always appears in Settings.
• Tap the photo viewer to hide its buttons.
• Tapping the avatar beside a photo opens that person's profile.
• The unread dot in the chat list is blue and smaller.
• Leave Chat stays clear of the bottom edge while you scroll a profile.
• The attach menu, card, and camera preview work reliably again.
• System messages appear in chats.
• Opening a group's profile from an invite card works.
• The mention picker works when the cursor sits inside an emoji or accented character.
• Link previews in the composer load for restored drafts and come back if you edit the link out.
• The reply strip no longer shows through the jump-to-bottom button.
• Unselected tab icons and labels are white on every iOS version.
• Sharing some web links into Flipcash works.
• The share extension and some captions use the app font.

Baseline — always verify these core money flows:
• Claim a Cash Link on an empty account, then buy a currency with the received balance
• Add money to your balance (Phantom or Coinbase)
• Scan & Send between two devices
• Expand a chat notification — the rich transcript renders and Reply works

Report anything off — wrong amounts, stuck screens, or crashes.
