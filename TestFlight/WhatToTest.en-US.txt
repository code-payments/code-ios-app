What to test in this build

New in this release — please exercise these end to end:
• Launch the app — it should open on the Chat tab
• Leave a chat with unread messages and come back — a divider should mark where the unread messages start
• Scroll through a chat — messages should mark as read only once they've been on screen
• In a group, have someone type — their avatar should appear ahead of the typing indicator
• Send a cash link into a chat and have it collected — the sender should get a thank-you
• Open a group you qualify for but haven't joined — you should be able to read the conversation
• Open a group member's profile and tap Message
• Send a link into a chat and share a group invite — each should get its own card
• Join a group with a balance requirement by buying exactly the required amount — it should unlock
• From a gated group chat, buy, deposit, or convert — you should land back in that chat
• Scan a group invite QR — it should open the invite
• Open a Flipcash universal link from outside the app — it should take you to the right place
• On the scanner, the gallery button sits at the top right on its glass surface

Baseline — always verify these core money flows:
• Claim a Cash Link on an empty account, then buy a currency with the received balance
• Add money to your balance (Phantom or Coinbase)
• Scan & Send between two devices
• Expand a chat notification — the rich transcript renders and Reply works

Report anything off — wrong amounts, stuck screens, or crashes.
