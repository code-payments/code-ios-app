What to test in this build

New in this release:
• The You tab now has a tip card — open it, check the name scales to fit and the card matches the design
• Deposit USDC — the conversion graphic should read the right way round
• Buy a currency — the Get receipt should lead with what you pay
• Enter/change your display name — check the title and hint read clearly
• Scan a Cash Link or code — detection should be quick and reliable
• Switch accounts — your tutorial should stay marked complete
• Enter a max amount on the keypad — the maximum offered should already account for fees
• Push into token info, then swipe back from the screen edge — the back gesture should work and the token name should not overlap the back button

Baseline — always verify these core money flows:
• Claim a Cash Link on an empty account, then buy a currency with the received balance
• Add money to your balance (Phantom or Coinbase)
• Scan & Send between two devices
• Expand a chat notification — the rich transcript renders and Reply works

Report anything off — wrong amounts, stuck screens, or crashes.
