What to test in this build

New in this release — verify each end to end:
• The You tab is now your own profile, with Settings behind the gear
• Edit your profile, including a bio and cover picture
• View other people's profiles, and open one before you've messaged them
• Send photos in chats, encrypted in private messages
• Double-tap a photo to react to it
• Favorite public groups from their profiles
• Groups can have a cover picture and description
• A group's profile links to its token and shows it in Balance Requirements
• A cleaner one-row message composer, with Send Cash on the right
• Redesigned group profile and Edit Group screens
• Long-press anywhere in a reaction row to see who reacted
• Convert is disabled when there's nothing to convert into
• Chats refresh correctly when you return to the app

Baseline — always verify these core money flows:
• Claim a Cash Link on an empty account, then buy a currency with the received balance
• Add money to your balance (Phantom or Coinbase)
• Scan & Send between two devices
• Expand a chat notification — the rich transcript renders and Reply works

Report anything off — wrong amounts, stuck screens, or crashes.
