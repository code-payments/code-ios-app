What to test in this build

New in this release — exercise each end to end:
• Send and receive tips directly in the app
• Create a profile in the new Tips tab and share your tip card
• On a NEW account: create your tip card, close Tips, reopen it — you should land on your tips list, not the setup screen
• Open a tip chat and confirm the keyboard is ready to reply
• Profile pictures load with progressive placeholders
• Swipe back on a pushed sheet screen (drag from the edge)
• Tap outside the keyboard in a chat to dismiss it
• Tap a cash bubble in a chat and confirm the right action fires

Baseline — always verify these core money flows:
• Claim a Cash Link on an empty account, then buy a currency with the received balance
• Add money to your balance (Phantom or Coinbase)
• Scan & Send between two devices
• Expand a chat notification — the rich transcript renders and Reply works

Report anything off — wrong amounts, stuck screens, or crashes.
