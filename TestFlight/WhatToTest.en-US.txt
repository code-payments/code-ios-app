What to test in this build

New in this release:
• Claim a username once your total Flipcash balance reaches $100, then open your tip card link and check people can find you by name
• Start a chat with someone by entering their Flipcash username
• Chat backfills messages you missed once your feed syncs
• In-chat payments read as sends rather than tips
• Discovery calls community holders members
• The email AutoFill suggestion is back on the verification step
• Saving a name confirms with the same checkmark the rest of the app uses
• Currencies with a zero balance stay out of the withdraw picker
• Negative amounts put the minus ahead of the currency symbol

Baseline — always verify these core money flows:
• Claim a Cash Link on an empty account, then buy a currency with the received balance
• Add money to your balance (Phantom or Coinbase)
• Scan & Send between two devices
• Expand a chat notification — the rich transcript renders and Reply works

Report anything off — wrong amounts, stuck screens, or crashes.
