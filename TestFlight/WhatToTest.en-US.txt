What to test in this build

New in this release:
• Add money and go through identity verification — the flow should stay on screen from start to finish, with no blank screen partway through
• Open a link to your own tip card, or scan your own tipcode — either should land on the You tab showing your card, with any open sheet dismissed
• Follow a tip link with the keyboard up — the keyboard should go down as the link opens
• Open the tip card full screen on the You tab, then pull it up — it should track your finger back into its slot; a short pull, or a downward one, should spring back
• Scroll the wallet to the bottom — the last tile should sit a normal gap above the tab bar, not a large empty space

Baseline — always verify these core money flows:
• Claim a Cash Link on an empty account, then buy a currency with the received balance
• Add money to your balance (Phantom or Coinbase)
• Scan & Send between two devices
• Expand a chat notification — the rich transcript renders and Reply works

Report anything off — wrong amounts, stuck screens, or crashes.
