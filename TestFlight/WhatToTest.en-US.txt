What to test in this build

New in this release — please exercise these end to end:
• Create a public group, join one, and edit a group's name and picture
• Check who's in a group from the group info at the top of the chat
• Report a message, a person, and a group
• Mute a chat, then confirm it stays quiet
• Scan a code from a photo already in your gallery
• On the scanner, the gallery button sits at the top right on its glass surface
• Send a cash link and a token link into a chat — both should appear as cards you can act on
• Start typing in a chat, leave, and come back — the draft should still be there
• Scan a tipcard — it should land in the chat, not a tip sheet
• Open a Flipcash link or QR from outside the app; a link on any other host should be rejected
• Swipe to reply — it should arm only on the bubble and send when you lift

Baseline — always verify these core money flows:
• Claim a Cash Link on an empty account, then buy a currency with the received balance
• Add money to your balance (Phantom or Coinbase)
• Scan & Send between two devices
• Expand a chat notification — the rich transcript renders and Reply works

Report anything off — wrong amounts, stuck screens, or crashes.
